Read this first

Collecting data from LinkedIn by automation conflicts with their User Agreement. Accounts and Sales Navigator seats do get restricted, and sometimes closed for good, over exactly this. No tool can engineer that risk away — including this one.

What it does do is keep the footprint small: it only reads data the page has already loaded for you, never requesting anything extra. It holds itself to strict hourly and daily ceilings, slows down when LinkedIn signals it should, and shuts off entirely at the first sign of a verification prompt.

Treat it as reduced risk, not safety. Run it on a seat you could stand to lose, and remember that the personal data it collects carries obligations under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws wherever your contacts live.
